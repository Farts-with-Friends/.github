## What is Farts with Friends?

Farts with Friends is a social audio app built for sharing the sounds that make life funny.

Record a short audio blip, choose who can hear it, and discover recordings from your friends or the wider community. Rate each sound, compete on leaderboards, earn badges, and settle the important question: who has the most legendary sound?

Farts with Friends connects the same community across iOS and Android.

## ✨ Features

- 🎙️ **Record Audio Blips** — Capture recordings up to 20 seconds long
- 🔒 **Choose Your Audience** — Keep a recording private or share it with selected friends, groups, all friends, or the public community
- 👥 **Friends and Friend Requests** — Find people, connect with friends, and follow their latest recordings
- 🫂 **Friend Groups** — Create groups, invite friends with a code, and share recordings with specific circles
- 🌎 **Community Feed** — Discover public sounds from the wider community
- 🔥 **Trending Sounds** — See which recordings are getting the community's attention
- 🔟 **Detailed Ratings** — Score sounds from 1–10 for overall quality, length, volume, tone, and juiciness
- 🏆 **Leaderboards** — Compete in global, category, and group leaderboards
- 🎖️ **Badges and Achievements** — Unlock badges by recording, receiving ratings, making friends, and reaching milestones
- 🔔 **Notifications** — Get updates about friend requests, new recordings, ratings, group invitations, and badges
- 📱 **Cross-Platform Community** — Connect with people using either iOS or Android
- 🛡️ **Safety Tools** — Report content, block accounts, manage visibility, and contact support from inside the app
- 📇 **Optional Contact Discovery** — Find existing friends without uploading or storing your complete address book
- 🌙 **Light and Dark Themes** — Choose the appearance that works best for you

## 🚀 How It Works

1. Create your account and choose a username.
2. Record a sound up to 20 seconds long.
3. Choose who is allowed to hear it.
4. Listen to recordings from friends or explore the public community.
5. Submit your 1–10 ratings.
6. Earn badges and climb the leaderboards.

> Ratings are final once submitted, so judge wisely.

## 🔐 Sharing and Privacy

Every recording has audience controls. You can share with:

- **Only Me** — Stored privately for your account
- **Selected Friends** — Shared only with the people you choose
- **Groups** — Shared with members of selected friend groups
- **All Friends** — Available to everyone on your friends list
- **Public Community** — Eligible to appear in community feeds, ratings, and leaderboards

We do not sell your personal information. Read the complete [Privacy Policy](https://fartswithfriends.com/privacy) for more information.

## 🛠️ Built With

Farts with Friends is built as a native experience on both platforms:

- **iOS:** Swift and SwiftUI
- **Android:** Kotlin and Jetpack Compose
- **Cloud services:** Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, and push notifications
- **Subscriptions:** Apple StoreKit and Google Play Billing
