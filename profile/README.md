# Farts with Friends

**Record funny sounds. Share them with friends. See what gets the biggest laugh.**

A social audio app for iPhone, iPad, and Android. Use the same account across platforms and share with friends on either one.

## What you can do

- **Record and share.** Capture up to 20 seconds of audio. Keep it private, share it with all friends, select individual friends or groups, or post to the public community.
- **Find your friends.** Connect with a friend code or QR code, create groups, and invite friends into your circle.
- **Listen and react.** Explore sounds from friends and the wider community. React, comment, rate recordings, and save your favorites.
- **See how you rank.** Compare scores on friend, group, and public leaderboards, earn badges, and track your recording streak.
