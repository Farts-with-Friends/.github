## What is Farts with Friends?

Farts with Friends is the social audio app you never knew you needed. Record short audio blips, share them with friends, and let the world rate your masterpieces on a 5-star scale. Climb the global leaderboard, earn badges, and prove once and for all who reigns supreme.

## ✨ Features

- 🎙️ **Record Blips** — Capture audio clips up to 20 seconds
- 👥 **Share with Friends** — Send your blips to your inner circle
- ⭐ **5-Star Ratings** — Let the community judge your work
- 🏆 **Global Leaderboard** — Compete for the top spot and earn badges
