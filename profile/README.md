## Overview

Farts with Friends is a social audio app for iPhone, iPad, and Android. Record a sound, share it with the people you choose, and listen to recordings from friends or the wider community.

The same accounts, friends, and recordings work across both platforms.

## Features

- **Recording.** Capture a sound up to 20 seconds long.
- **Audience controls.** Keep a recording private, share it with all friends, selected friends, selected groups, or the public community.
- **Friends.** Send and accept friend requests, browse profiles, and connect with a friend code or QR code.
- **Groups.** Create groups, invite members, and share recordings with a specific circle.
- **Community.** Discover public recordings from people outside your friends list.
- **Reactions and comments.** Respond to a recording, or leave a comment.
- **Ratings.** Score a recording from 1 to 10 on overall quality, duration, volume, tone, and clarity. On Android, clarity is labeled Juiciness.
- **Leaderboards.** Compare recordings across friends, groups, categories, and the public community.
- **Badges.** Unlock badges for recording, ratings, friends, and other milestones.
- **Notifications.** Get updates for friend requests, new recordings, ratings, comments, group invitations, and badges.
- **Appearance.** Use light mode, dark mode, or match the device setting.
- **Safety tools.** Report content, block accounts, and contact support from inside the app.
- **Contact discovery.** Optionally find people you already know. Matching runs on the device. The app does not upload your address book.

## How it works

1. Create an account and choose a username.
2. Record a sound up to 20 seconds long.
3. Choose who can hear it.
4. Listen to recordings from friends, groups, or the public community.
5. React, comment, and submit a rating from 1 to 10.
6. Earn badges and appear on leaderboards.

A rating can be edited for 10 minutes after it is submitted. After that, it is locked.

## Sharing and privacy

Each recording has its own audience:

- **Only me.** Visible only on your account.
- **Friends.** Available to everyone on your friends list.
- **Selected friends.** Shared only with the people you choose.
- **Groups.** Shared with members of the groups you select.
- **Public community.** Eligible to appear in community feeds, ratings, and leaderboards.

Farts with Friends does not sell personal information. Details are in the [Privacy Policy](https://fartswithfriends.com/privacy).
